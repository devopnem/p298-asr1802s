Here's how to enable telnet with "Modem Command".
Make sure you turn off the PIPE Mode to complete the command.
Enter the command below in "Modem Command".
curl -k https://www.jiwanyuzu.com/download/software/busybox/busybox-armv7l -o /root/busybox

chmod 755 /root/busybox

/root/busybox telnetd -l /bin/ash
