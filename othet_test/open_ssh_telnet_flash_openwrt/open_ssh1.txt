Here’s my other workaround to access SSH connection.

Use "nc" to download the file.
(Tab 1) ./busybox nc -l -l 2026 > dropbear.tz
(Tab 2) nc 192.168.8.1 2026 < dropbear.tgz
(Tab 3) tar -xzf dropbear.tgz
(Tab 3) ppf ./ssh/* /
(Tab 3) reboot
